How - MIN SAFE GAP - High-school plain English - hire seats first - then 7shifts
💡 Count real guests -> how many servers each shift -> how many people on payroll -> who is missing -> hire them -> build the week in 7shifts.
What are we calculating?
MIN SAFE CURRENT GAP by role = how many seats to hire
What is this NOT?
Not the final 7shifts board. That is publish later.
Foundation role
Server - everyone else hangs off the server grid
1 📊 POS guests->2 🍽️ div30->3 📈 T/MIN/SAFE->4 👥 Host Bus Boss->5 🔧 GAP hire->6 📱 7shifts
1
📊 Phase 1 - Average guests
Build 14 separate averages - Mon AM with Mon AM, Mon PM with Mon PM ... Sun PM with Sun PM. Never one number for the whole week. Last 4-8 weeks. Split AM/PM at 4:30 PM ET.
2
🍽️ Phase 2 - Servers on floor
One server handles max 30 guests per shift. For each of 14 shifts: ROUNDUP(avg / 30). Example: 150 -> 5, 240 -> 8.
3
👥 Phase 3 - How many to employ
T = sum(14) MIN = ROUNDUP(T/4) SAFE = ROUNDUP((T/4)x1.25) GAP = SAFE - CURRENT
Example: T=76 -> MIN 19 SAFE 24 if CURRENT 19 -> hire 5
4
👔 Phase 4 - Other roles
Host - 1 door, 2 Fri/Sat PM (or servers >=6)
Bus - ~1 per 3-4 servers on rush
GM - 1 @ 55h Wed/Sun OFF
AGM - 1 @ 6sh/36h
FM - FM1 6sh/36h + FM2 2sh/12h
Hard rule: every open hour = GM or AGM or Floor. Lead != boss.
5
🔧 Hire the GAP
If GAP > 0 -> recruit that role. Do not invent fake names on the board.
6
📅 Build and publish
Put real names in 7shifts. Build order: Boss -> Host -> Server -> Bus. Publish by Friday 5pm.

Company Policies - From Brain - All stores - All brands
Same rules every store. Read them. Follow them. Ask @WISE OPS if unsure.
TEAM-003 . 1 - NON-NEGOTIABLE - 1 · NON-NEGOTIABLE:...
1 · NON-NEGOTIABLE:
1.1 Theft — no stealing money, food, supplies.
1.2 Comping — no free items without manager approval.
1.3 Fraud — never fake time, edits, or records.
1.4 Harassment — no discrimination or harassment.
1.5 Substances — no drugs or alcohol on shift.
1.6 No-Show — missing shift without calling + discipline.
1.7 Violence — no threats, weapons, or fighting.
TEAM-003 . 2 - ATTENDANCE - 2 · ATTENDANCE:...
2 · ATTENDANCE:
2.1 Coverage — work your shifts or find coverage.
2.2 Clocking — clock in/out at correct times.
2.3 Callout — call out 4+ hours early.
2.4 Illness — stay home if sick (vomiting/fever).
2.5 Limit — 3 callouts in 30 days = discipline.
TEAM-003 . 3 - PAY & TIPS - 3 · PAY & TIPS:...
3 · PAY & TIPS:
3.1 Hours — record every minute accurately.

Tip-Out Policy

WHO: Servers, hosts, and bussers. The Server Lead runs the math.

WHEN: Every night at close.

PURPOSE: Same tip-out math, every store, every night.

SCOPE: All brands. All stores. Every tipped shift.

RESPONSIBILITY: Servers report sales. Server Lead does the math. Closing manager checks.

THE RULE:
1. Servers tip out 1% of net sales — 0.5% to hosts, 0.5% to bussers.
2. Net sales — food and drink after discounts. Tax not counted.
3. More than one host or busser? Split that share by hours worked.
4. No host or busser on shift? That share stays with the servers. 5. THE FLOOR: a server never tips out more than the tips they collected that shift. If 1% of sales is bigger than their tips, the tip-out stops at their tips — it can never go negative. The payroll robot enforces this automatically.

PROCEDURE:
1. Print each server's net sales at close.
2. Write every number on the Tip-Out Sheet. No verbal deals.
3. Split the host and busser shares by hours worked.
4. Pay each person their share the same night.
5. Everyone who pays or gets paid signs the sheet.
6. Closing manager checks the math, signs, and files the sheet.

WHAT IF: Math disputed? Freeze it. The closing manager decides tonight. The rule never bends.
